Step-by-Step Guide

Your Path to NRC

1. Online Application

Start by creating an account on our admission portal. Fill out the comprehensive application form with your personal details, educational background, and program preferences.

2. Document Submission

Upload high-quality scanned copies of all required documents. Ensure your academic transcripts, identification, and certificates meet our formatting guidelines.

3. Interview & Review

Shortlisted candidates will be invited for an online or in-person interview. Our admissions committee will holistically review your application and interview performance.

4. Final Enrollment

Receive your official offer letter. Complete the fee payment, confirm your seat, and get ready to join the vibrant student community at our Paro campus!

Required Documents

Class XII Transcripts

CID / Passport

Clear copy of Citizenship Identity Card (for Bhutanese) or Passport (for International).

Recent Photographs

Two recent passport-sized photographs with a white background.

School Leaving Certificate

Transfer certificate or character certificate from the last school attended.
